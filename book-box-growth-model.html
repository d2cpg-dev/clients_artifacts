    Model notes for the client:

    · Attribution: all sessions and conversions are assumed to come from contributors who received and clicked the thank-you email. Traffic from other sources is not modeled (paid media is estimated separately in the gap table).

    · “Sessions” are Shopify sessions, not unique visitors — one person can generate multiple sessions. In the contributor-wide scenario, 100% sessions per contributor means every contributor is exposed to the offer inside the donation flow.

    · Donations themselves are not counted as revenue here; the model covers book box subscriptions only.

    · Event counts are annual and divided by 12 for the monthly model.

    · Revenue math: New subs = Contributors × Sessions rate × Order CVR · Active(t) = Active(t−1) × (1 − churn) + New subs · MRR = Active × ARPU. Paid budget = extra subscribers needed × CAC.
